EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
-----------------
Welcome:
Welcome to the company. We are committed to creating a productive work environment.

Work Culture:
We encourage teamwork, innovation, and continuous learning.

Performance Review:
Employee performance is reviewed twice a year.

Training:
Employees are encouraged to participate in training programs.

Growth Opportunities:
Employees will have opportunities for career advancement based on performance.


CODE OF CONDUCT
---------------
Professional Behavior:
Employees must behave respectfully with colleagues and clients.

Harassment Policy:
Any form of harassment is strictly prohibited.

Conflict of Interest:
Employees must disclose any conflicts of interest.

Company Property:
Employees must handle company assets responsibly.

Disciplinary Actions:
Violation of policies may result in warnings, suspension, or termination.


HR POLICY
---------
Working Hours:
Employees are expected to work 9 hours per day, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, including a 1-hour break.

Attendance:
Employees must mark attendance daily using the company system. Late arrival beyond 15 minutes will be recorded.

Dress Code:
Employees are required to wear formal attire from Monday to Friday. Casual wear is allowed on Saturdays.

Remote Work:
Employees are allowed to work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Code of Behavior:
All employees must maintain professional conduct and respect workplace ethics at all times.


IT SECURITY POLICY
------------------
Password Policy:
Employees must use strong passwords and change them every 60 days.

Data Protection:
Confidential company data must not be shared outside the organization.

Device Usage:
Only authorized devices should be used to access company systems.

Email Usage:
Employees must not open suspicious email attachments or links.

Security Violations:
Any security breach must be reported immediately to the IT department.


LEAVE POLICY
------------
Annual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 20 paid leaves per year.

Sick Leave:
Employees can take up to 10 sick leaves annually. A medical certificate may be required for more than 2 consecutive days.

Casual Leave:
Employees can take 5 casual leaves per year for personal reasons.

Leave Approval:
All leaves must be approved by the reporting manager in advance.

Unpaid Leave:
If paid leaves are exhausted, employees may apply for unpaid leave subject to approval.
